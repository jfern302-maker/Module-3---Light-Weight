Light Weight

The Burden

This object is a self-portrait of the human condition. It is a physical manifestation of our capacity to carry weight—both the literal and the psychological. In our nature, to be "on" is to be carrying something; the light we project is often the direct result of the pressure we endure.

"Visibility is the cost of carrying weight."

The lamp functions as a tactile task manager. Each pebble added is a commitment, a responsibility, a piece of life held in place. We find balance in the glow of our endurance, but the system is finite. When the burden exceeds the threshold of the individual, the logic fails, and the light fractures into a spasm. Only when the weight is removed can the light truly go out—a signal that the cycle is complete, and we are permitted to rest until the next burden arrives.

States of Capacity

01 Rest

The absence of weight. In this state, the light is extinguished, signaling a period of recovery and silence. The structure is at peace.

02 Endurance

The weight is held. The light is steady and full—a testament to the balance between the burden carried and the capacity to hold it.

03 Fracture

The limit is exceeded. The light can no longer maintain a steady state; it spasms erratically as a signal of internal breakdown.
